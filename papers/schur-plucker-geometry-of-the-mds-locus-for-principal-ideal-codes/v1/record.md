abstract: "Let \\(g\\) be a monic polynomial of degree \\(r<n\\), and let \\(C_g(n)\\) be the coefficient-vector code formed by multiples \\(ug\\) with \\(\\deg(ug)<n\\). We study the coefficient-space maximum distance separable (MDS) locus \\(M_{n,r}\\) as \\(g\\) varies. Coefficient space is realized as the quotient of cyclic matrix--vector pairs by simultaneous change of basis, with the companion pair as a global slice, and the remainder-orbit map embeds it as a smooth closed complete intersection in the standard big cell of \\(\\operatorname{Gr}(r,n)\\). Every normalized maximal Pl\\\"ucker coordinate pulls back, up to sign, to an explicit power of the constant coefficient \\(A_0\\) times a Schur polynomial \\(S_\\kappa(g)=s_\\kappa(\\Lambda_g)\\), with \\(\\kappa\\subseteq (n-r)^{r-1}\\). Hence\n$$\nD_{n,r}=A_0\\prod_{\\kappa\\subseteq (n-r)^{r-1}}S_\\kappa\n$$\nis the exact universal MDS polynomial. This description yields a flat non-MDS Cartier boundary over \\(\\mathbb Z\\), exact degrees and Pl\\\"ucker multiplicities, scaling and reciprocal symmetries, and a nested length filtration by first-failure layers. It gives an exact content criterion on root-multiplicity strata, density-one results on irreducible Frobenius strata, and the identity \\(\\sigma_r(g)=r+\\tau(g)-1\\), relating the first sparse multiple to the first vanishing Schur width. Over finite fields we also determine the companion projective period from root ratios and multiplicities. For \\(r\\ge3\\) and \\(N\\ge r+3\\), every nonempty first-failure layer over an algebraically closed field has a dense open locus of MDS codes that are not of generalized Reed--Solomon type."
