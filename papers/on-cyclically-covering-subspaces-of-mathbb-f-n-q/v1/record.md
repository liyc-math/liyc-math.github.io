abstract: "For a prime power $q$ and a positive integer $n$, let $h_q(n)$ be the largest codimension of a subspace of $\\mathbb F_q^n$ whose cyclic shifts cover the whole space. Using invariant decompositions and trace pairings, we prove that $h_q(\\ell^t)=0$ whenever $q>2$ is a primitive root modulo the odd prime power $\\ell^t$. We also show that $h_q(2n)=0$ if and only if $h_q(n)=0$ when $q$ and $n$ are odd. Multiplicative-subgroup constructions yield further exact values, and we establish $h_3(11)=h_3(16)=1$. Finally, we prove $h_{q^m}(n)\\leq h_q(n)$, so every zero value is preserved under field extension."
