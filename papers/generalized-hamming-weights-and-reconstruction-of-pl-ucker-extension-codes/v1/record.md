title: "Generalized Hamming Weights and Reconstruction of Pl\\\"ucker Extension Codes"

abstract: "Let $U\\le\\F_q^n$ have dimension $k-1$, where $1\\le k\\le n$. As a row appended to a generator matrix of $U$ varies, the vectors of maximal minors form a linear code $\\cP(U)$ of length $\\binom{n}{k}$ and dimension $t=n-k+1$. We show that $\\cP(U)$ is monomially equivalent to the full Jurrius--Pellikaan derived code of $U^\\perp$. For $2\\le k\\le n-1$ and $(k-1)$-dimensional seeds $U,U'\\le\\F_q^n$, we prove that $\\cP(U)$ and $\\cP(U')$ are monomially equivalent if and only if $U$ and $U'$ are monomially equivalent. For maximum distance separable seeds in this range, we determine the complete generalized Hamming weight hierarchy and classify all subcodes attaining each generalized weight."
keywords: "Generalized Hamming weights, linear codes, Pl\\\"ucker extension codes, reconstruction."
