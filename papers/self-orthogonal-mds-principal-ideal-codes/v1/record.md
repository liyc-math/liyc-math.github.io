abstract: "We investigate Euclidean self-orthogonality and the generalized Reed--Solomon (GRS) property for coefficient-vector codes generated by a monic polynomial $g$ and its shifts $xg,\\ldots,x^{k-1}g$, of length $n=r+k$ and dimension $k$, where $\\deg g=r$ and $3\\le k\\le r$. We give a complete classification, over any algebraically closed field, of the generators for which these codes are both self-orthogonal and GRS. Such generators have either distinct roots in a geometric progression satisfying explicit order and scalar conditions, or a single repeated root in certain positive characteristics. In particular, only finitely many generators occur for fixed $r$, $k$, and characteristic.
